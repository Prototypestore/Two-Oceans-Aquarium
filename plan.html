02 · THE PLAN

The Field Trip

THE INTRODUCTION

A hands-on Life Sciences experience

The field trip to the Two Oceans Aquarium in Cape Town will take place over one week, during which 25 learners and 5 teachers will embark on an educational adventure to explore symbiotic interactions, sustained ocean biodiversity, and predator-prey dynamics.

The purpose of this task is to bring the Life Sciences curriculum to life through a memorable, hands-on experience that goes beyond the classroom.

There will be a total of 25 Grade 12 learners and 5 teachers. There will be a total of 5 groups, each consisting of 5 learners and one teacher.

The trip will take place in Cape Town for one week, and we will be accommodated in a lodge near the Two Oceans Aquarium.

The field trip will connect classroom learning with real-world application and help learners become more informed and responsible about marine ecosystems.
