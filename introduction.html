FIELD TRIP SUMMARY

Summary of the field trip: ‘Two Oceans Aquarium’

The summary will include the following: the estimated total budget for each student, additional personal costs for each student, transport costs, accommodation costs, and the necessary dietary and allergy requirements to ensure a safe and fun trip.

There will be a total of 25 Grade 12 learners and 5 teachers. There will be a total of 5 groups, each consisting of 5 learners and one teacher. The trip will take place in Cape Town for one week, and we will be accommodated in a lodge near the Two Oceans Aquarium.
