03 · LEARNING ACTIVITIES

Learning in Practice

Through guided observation, note-taking, and interactive activities such as species identification and habitat mapping, learners will actively engage in the practical process, developing critical thinking skills, curiosity, and environmental awareness.

Species identification

During the trip, learners will observe a variety of marine species and learn about their behaviours, adaptations, habitats, and complex bio-ecosystems such as coral reefs and kelp forests.

Habitat mapping

Through guided observation, note-taking, and interactive activities such as species identification and habitat mapping, learners will actively engage in the practical process.

Adaptations

Learners will also examine how environmental factors like water temperature, salinity, and human impact affect marine life adaptation.

Predator-prey dynamics

Learners will compare marine and land animals, discover adaptation patterns, and learn about predator-prey dynamics.

Symbiotic interactions

Learners will learn about symbiotic interactions and the connection between animals and their environments.

Environmental factors

Learners will examine how environmental factors like water temperature, salinity, and human impact affect marine life adaptation.

These activities are important because they help students better understand animals, biomes, and habitats, as well as the connection between animals and their environments.

The learners will also work in their groups to complete the activities, with the teacher in each group providing guidance and supervision.
