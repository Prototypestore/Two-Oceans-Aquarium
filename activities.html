02 · ACTIVITIES

Planning for Safety

The 25 learners will be divided into 5 groups of 5, with one teacher assigned to each group to ensure guidance and supervision throughout. Both learners and teachers will be housed in lodging near the aquarium for the duration of the trip.

Transport

The group will travel from Port Elizabeth to Cape Town, and the travel time will be approximately 1 hour 30 minutes to 3 hours. The teachers will plan the transport and any stops that may be needed, especially for students who do not do well travelling long distances.

Accommodation

The accommodation will cost around R300-R600 per night, and a back-up plan will be made in case the accommodation is cancelled.

Both learners and teachers will be housed in lodging near the aquarium for the duration of the trip.

Food and dietary requirements

There will also be a food budget, and the teachers will make sure that food allergies are accommodated for and that everyone gets food.

The total cost will include transport, entry fees, materials, accommodation, and food, and the teachers will plan accordingly to make sure there is enough money for the trip.

QUESTIONNAIRE AND GRAPHS

Dietary and Allergy Requirements

Before going on the trip I made a questionnaire and a few graphs to get a better understanding of the students who we will be travelling with on the trip, and what we as the teachers must look out for.

Furthermore, on the questionnaire and graphs we asked questions about the students' dietary requirements and allergy requirements.

Additionally, the purpose for recording this information is to avoid any casualties and keep the students safe while enjoying their time on their trip.

These recorded results will also be forwarded to our accommodation to inform the kitchen staff of these students and to provide a variety of food dishes.

SAFETY

Safety and Supervision

The learners will also work in their groups to complete the activities, with the teacher in each group providing guidance and supervision.

Safety training and speech

Safety training and a safety speech will be given before the activities, explaining why staying safe when around wild animals is essential.

Risk assessment

The teachers will also do risk assessments and increase supervision to prevent any unnecessary injuries.

Risks considered

Risks such as animal attacks, injuries, weather conditions, and getting lost will be considered when planning the trip. Supervision is a necessity for safety throughout the field trip.
