05 · THE SOLUTION

Learning Beyond
the Classroom

Beyond scientific knowledge, the trip is designed to nurture personal skills such as teamwork, responsibility, and respect for nature.

Observing marine animals in their natural environments allows students to better understand their characteristics and behaviours, fostering empathy and a stronger commitment to conservation.

Furthermore, this experience is to help learners use what they have learned in the future by making more informed decisions about protecting marine ecosystems and understanding the connection between animals and their environments.

After the trip, learners can reflect on what they have learned and evaluate how the experience has improved their knowledge, teamwork, responsibility, and respect for nature.
